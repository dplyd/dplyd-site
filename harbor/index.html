FAQ

Straight answers for partners.

Q ▸ Who owns the customer relationship in a Harbor deployment?

You do. Harbor is infrastructure, not resale. You keep the contract, the pricing, the product roadmap, and the account. DPLYD is the deployment and management layer underneath it.

Q ▸ Does my product have to be rebuilt to run on the appliance?

No. If your product runs in containers against an OpenAI-compatible inference endpoint, it runs on a Harbor appliance. We scope the specifics — compute, storage, data flow, network egress — before any hardware ships.

Q ▸ Which models can we run?

Current-generation open-weight models. We select one with your team based on your workload's performance, cost, and compliance requirements rather than defaulting to whatever is popular. Model choice never requires sending customer data off-premises.

Q ▸ Who does the customer call when something breaks?

DPLYD owns the appliance: hardware, operating system, inference runtime, updates, and monitoring. You own your application. The split is clean, and we support your team directly rather than putting you in the middle of hardware tickets.

Q ▸ How does Harbor pricing work for partners?

A fixed cost per deployed appliance — no per-token metering and no per-seat licensing — so your margin does not erode as a customer's usage grows. Exact pricing depends on deployment size and volume commitment.

Q ▸ Can our customers run disconnected or air-gapped?

Yes. The appliance can run fully disconnected, with managed updates delivered on a schedule and mechanism that fits the customer's environment, including offline transfer for air-gapped enclaves.
