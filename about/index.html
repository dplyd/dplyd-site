The company behind the appliance.

DPLYD AI LLC builds fully managed on-premises AI appliances for organizations whose data can't leave the building — law firms, health systems, defense contractors, and school districts.

DPLYD was founded by Kendall Miller to close the gap between frontier AI productivity and the data-handling obligations of regulated organizations. The founding observation: every "compliant" cloud AI product still asks you to ship your most sensitive content — or an index derived from it — to somebody else's computer. DPLYD's answer is to move the computer instead.

We install and operate the hardware; you own the boundary. Every demo is given by the founder, on your own data.
