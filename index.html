01 / The Tension

The AI compromise you shouldn't have to make.

Frontier AI means shipping your most sensitive information to a third-party API. For law firms, clinics, districts, and defense contractors, that trade is a non-starter.

▚

The architecture you built to protect your assets was right then. It's still right today.

The hidden leak

Keeping the file local isn't enough. The index is the leak.

Most "on-prem-friendly" AI still ships an index to the cloud. But an index derived from your document is a second copy of it — queryable, correlatable, and often reversible. The original never moved; the sensitive information did.

Read the full explainer →
local document → content extraction → cloud index · outside your boundary
KEYWORDS

Topics, names, projects, clients, and legal matters — right there in plaintext.

CHUNKS

Verbatim passages copied straight out of the file. Not derived — the content itself.

EMBEDDINGS

Encode the meaning — and are reversible enough to reconstruct the source text.

METADATA

Filenames, authors, dates, case IDs, and paths — a map of what you hold.

RELATIONSHIPS

Which people, entities, and documents connect — disclosure without a single sentence.

◆

A cloud index of local content is exfiltration the moment it holds enough derived information to disclose, infer, search, or reconstruct protected content outside your walls. DPLYD keeps the index on the same side of the boundary as the data.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Q ▸ Does any data leave my network?

No. The model, the retrieval index, the embeddings, and the logs all live on the appliance inside your perimeter. Nothing derived from your documents is transmitted to a third-party API.

Q ▸ What is a sovereign AI appliance?

A managed hardware unit we install on your premises that runs generative AI models locally. You get the productivity of modern AI; your data never crosses your network boundary.

Q ▸ How is this different from a private cloud or GovCloud AI service?

GovCloud and "private cloud" AI is still someone else's computer — your content and its search index are processed and stored outside your walls, under someone else's controls. DPLYD moves the computer to you instead.

Q ▸ What does it cost?

One fixed monthly fee per appliance covering hardware, models, updates, monitoring, and support. No per-seat licenses, no per-token metering, no capex. Exact pricing depends on headcount and workload.

Q ▸ Who installs and maintains the hardware?

We do. DPLYD delivers, installs, monitors, patches, and refreshes the appliance as a managed service. Your IT team keeps control of the network boundary; we keep the box healthy.

Q ▸ What models run on the appliance?

Current-generation open-weight models selected and tuned for your workload, updated by us as the state of the art moves. Model choice never requires sending data off-prem.
