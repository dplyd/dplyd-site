Last December was the AI wow moment. The frontier labs shipped models that were finally good enough that the whole tech world said, "Oh, this is different." Engineers had watched the progress for years and been impressed. But December was when everything changed.

That same moment has now arrived for open weight models.

Over the last few months, the open models caught up to where it counts. They're good enough to handle the vast majority of real work, provided you have decent hardware and a proper setup. And you no longer need a rack of servers to run them well. The hardware requirement has collapsed. What used to demand a data center now fits on a desk, in a small box.

That collapse solves a problem that has kept AI out of the industries that need it most.

If you work in healthcare, defense, legal, or finance, you already know the calculation. The capability and productivity gains of AI were tempting, but the privacy risk was disqualifying. Sending your data, your legal briefs, your patient records, your P&Ls to someone else's cloud was never going to fly with your compliance team, and rightly so. So you waited.

You don't have to wait anymore. When the model runs locally, the privacy concern goes away because the data goes nowhere. It stays on your box, in your office, under your control. Nothing leaks because nothing leaves.

That's the whole reason DPLYD exists.

We put great open weight models on a small box that sits on your desk. One unit comfortably serves five to twenty people depending on how hard they lean on it.

But the model is the easy part. The hard part is everything around it, and that's where we spent our effort. DPLYD ships with auditability built in, so you can show your work when someone asks. Role based access controls, so only the right people see the right things. And the ability to load the models with your own local knowledge, so the system can respond with context specific to you.

Stability. Security. Your most sensitive data can now leverage AI, and trust it will never walk out the door.
